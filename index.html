⚗️ V60 Pour Over
Grind: Medium-Fine • Ratio: 1:16 • Temp: 93°C • Time: 3:00

Rinse paper filter with hot water. Add 18g coffee. Pour 50g bloom, swirl gently for 45s. Pour steadily in spirals up to 288g by 1:45. Let drawdown complete by 3:00.

🚀 AeroPress (Inverted)
Grind: Medium • Ratio: 1:14 • Temp: 88°C • Time: 2:00

Invert AeroPress. Add 16g coffee. Pour 220g water at 88°C. Stir 10 times. Attach cap with rinsed filter. At 1:30, flip onto server and press gently for 30s.

🫖 French Press Immersion
Grind: Coarse • Ratio: 1:15 • Temp: 95°C • Time: 4:30

Add 30g coarse ground coffee. Pour 450g near-boiling water. Place lid on without pressing. At 4:00, gently break the crust with a spoon, scoop foam, and press slowly.
